Maggie Recipe

Description

Maggie is very easy to make recipe that takes few ingredients to make it.

ingredients

  • Water
  • 2 packets of Maggie noodles

Steps

  1. Pour 1.5 cup of water into a pen or pot and bring it to boil
  2. Stir in the maggie masala tastemaker packet until it dissolves in the boiling water
  3. Break noddle cake slightly, then drop them into the boiling water
  4. Cook for about 2 mins in medium flame
