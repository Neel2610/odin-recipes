Sandwich Recipe

Description

Sandwich is very easy to make recipe that takes few ingredients to make it.

ingredients

  • Two slices of bread
  • Butter or cheese spread
  • Green chutney
  • Thin slices of cucumber, tomate and boiled potato
  • Chaat masala

Steps

  1. Lay two slices of bread flat on a clean plate or counter.
  2. Spread butter, mayonnaise, or green chutney evenly on one side of each bread slice.
  3. Place thin slices of cucumber and tomato neatly on one of the bread slices.
  4. Sprinkle a tiny bit of salt and pepper over the vegetables for extra taste.
  5. Put the second slice of bread on top. Press down gently. Cut the sandwich in half diagonally with a butter knife.
